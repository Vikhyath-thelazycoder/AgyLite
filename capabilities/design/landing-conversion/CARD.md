# design/landing-conversion — Landing conversion

Landing and marketing page conversion strategy (offer, audience, one action, page type, copy, objections).

- **Use when:** landing page copy, conversion rate, call to action, waitlist page, sales page
- **Activation:** on-demand · priority 50 · context L2
- **Needs:** runtime none
- **Loads:** 2 bundled files; entry points: landing-page-design
- **Done means:** one offer, one audience and one primary action are stated · no competing CTAs above the fold · at least one risk reversal · no invented prices, stats or testimonials
