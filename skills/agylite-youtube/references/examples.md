# YouTube: a good and a weak delivery

Both are for the same request: "Write the hook and three title options for my video about switching from a
laptop to a desktop for coding." The scores below are made up to show the shape; real ones come from the tools.

## Weak

> Here are some great options!
>
> **Hook:** "What if I told you that everything you know about coding setups is WRONG? In this video, I'm going
> to reveal the shocking truth that will change the way you work forever!"
>
> **Titles:**
> 1. I Switched to a Desktop and You Won't BELIEVE What Happened!
> 2. The SHOCKING Truth About Laptops for Coding
> 3. Why 99% of Developers Are Using the Wrong Computer
>
> These hooks score very high for engagement and should perform really well!

Why it is weak:

- "score very high" names no tool and no number. Nothing was run.
- The hook promises a "shocking truth" and that "everything you know is wrong". The video is about one person's
  switch; it cannot deliver that.
- "99% of developers" is an invented statistic.
- It is in nobody's voice. Any channel could have these lines, and many do.
- One version only. No iteration, and no word on whether a voice file exists.

## Good

> Hook and titles — `youtube/desktop-switch/hook-and-titles.md`
> Voice: the creator's `voice.md` (dry, first person, numbers before opinions)
> Scores (from the tools):
> - Hook, version 3 (final): hookscore 78. Version 1: 61, version 2: 70.
>   "My laptop compiled this project in four minutes. This desktop does it in fifty seconds, and it cost less.
>   Here is what I gave up to get that."
> - Titles (`agylite youtube title`, each with the thumbnail text "4:00 → 0:50"):
>   1. "I replaced my laptop with a desktop for coding. The compile times" — 74
>   2. "4 minutes to 50 seconds: laptop vs desktop for coding" — 71
>   3. "The desktop I should have bought two years ago" — 58
>
> Based on: the script draft (`youtube/desktop-switch/script.md`) and the transcript of the creator's last three
> videos (`agylite research video … --subs`) for the voice.
> For the creator to do: record; pick the title. I would use 1 with the thumbnail text above.
> Not checked: the two numbers (4:00 and 0:50) come from the script draft. Please confirm that they are your real
> measurements before they go into the title.
>
> *(output of `agylite used`)*

Why it is good:

- Every score is a tool's number for the exact text, and the earlier versions are shown.
- The hook opens with the creator's own measurement and promises what the video contains: the trade-off.
- The voice is stated with where it came from.
- The one thing that could make the title untrue is handed back to the creator to confirm.
